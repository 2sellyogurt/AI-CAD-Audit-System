# AI CAD Audit System

**AI-Powered Construction Drawing Review Platform**

Welcome to the AI CAD Audit System Wiki. This system leverages LLM (Large Language Model) and computer vision to automate the compliance checking of architectural and engineering drawings against Chinese national building codes (GB standards).

## Overview

- **Multi-Discipline**: 12 specialized review agents covering building, structure, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, fire protection, etc.
- **Code Compliance**: 100+ built-in checkpoints mapped to GB standards
- **DXF/DWG Parsing**: Automatic drawing extraction, frame splitting, grid splitting, and scale recognition
- **LLM-Powered**: Dual-route architecture: rule-based checking + vision-language model review
- **Cross-Discipline**: Detects conflicts between different engineering disciplines
- **Professional Reports**: Role-based review reports with detailed findings
